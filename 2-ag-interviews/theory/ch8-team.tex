\section*{Found in your team's code}

\begin{itemize}
\item \code{createThread} has no thread limit, and stack slots are never reused
  (\code{next\_stack\_slot\_++}).
\item Fixed by 15 Nov: a thread sleeping in \code{pthread\_join} no longer panics the kernel when
  the process ends; the \code{fork} child copies \code{user\_stack\_top\_} /
  \code{lowest\_mapped\_stack\_vpn\_} (before, it could not grow its stack); \code{loadPage} now
  runs under \code{process\_lock\_}.
\item Fixed on 18 Oct (\code{pthread-cancel-points}): a thread sleeping in \code{pthread\_join} can
  be cancelled --- \code{cancelThread} broadcasts, the wait loop checks the caller's flag.
\end{itemize}
